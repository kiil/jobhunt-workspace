# Unsolicited application: AI advisor

## Recipient

Harbour Museum of Modern Art\
Shore Road\
Eastbay

## Letter

Dear Harbour Museum,

I am writing unsolicited because I would like to be your independent AI advisor: someone who helps you decide where artificial intelligence makes the museum better and where it should be kept out entirely. Both matter equally, and it is rare for anyone to be paid to say the latter.

I should be honest that this is not only a professional interest. I live in Eastbay and visit the museum often. It is a place where I feel at home, and where I take my guests when I want to show them something I care about. That is exactly why I care that the museum continues well into the future, and that technology becomes something it chooses with care rather than something that seeps in.

In my daily work I advise boards and management on the strategic use of AI. Much of that work is teaching: I design and lead courses for staff and management, from the basic concepts to concrete everyday use. A house is rarely served by a technology only a few enthusiasts understand. The value comes when curators, educators, communications and administration can judge for themselves when the tool is relevant and when it is not.

I also build. As lead AI architect at The Northport Courier I am responsible for an editorial AI system in daily operation, where I have had to draw the line between what the machine drafts and what a person decides. That line cannot be drawn in a policy paper alone. It has to be built into the system.

There are places where AI can clearly help a museum: collection records and search across archives, translation and accessibility for an international audience, and much of the administrative work behind the scenes. And there are places where the answer should be no: where the meeting between visitor, work and place is the whole point. That quality is what the technology should protect, not optimise away.

I do not know whether you have a role like this, and it could be anything from a defined advisory task to something more permanent. I would be glad to tell you more over a coffee in the café.

## Closing

Kind regards,

**Robin Vale**
