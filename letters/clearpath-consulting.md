# Application: AI solutions consultant

## Recipient

Clearpath Consulting\
Northport

## Letter

Dear Clearpath,

Your clients are public and critical-infrastructure organisations with complex workflows and high demands for data security, governance and stable operations. That is exactly where generative AI stops being a technology question and becomes a question of workflows, responsibility and trust, and it is the kind of problem I have spent the last years on. I am applying because the meeting point between customer, business and product is where I work best.

I build AI systems in production. At The Northport Courier I have built editorial agent teams with specialised roles, automatic article generation with integrated source grounding and an internal RAG system for semantic search. For a large leasing company I integrated RAG-based text generation with their existing fleet management system. The common thread is the same as for your clients: the output must be traceable to its source, or it is not knowledge but noise with confidence.

The other half of the role — workshops, presentations and translating between disciplines — is what I have made my living from for two decades. I am trained as a chemist and science journalist, and today I advise boards and management on AI, run workshops and teach staff. I can sit in a room with specialists, digital consultants and an IT manager and get all three to understand the same solution, and I am sober enough to say when AI is not the answer.

What I would like to learn is implementation at scale: how a platform is rolled out across many disciplines in an organisation with real demands for security and operations, and how customer feedback becomes product improvements instead of staying in a support email.

I would be glad to show some of what I have built in an interview.

## Closing

Kind regards,

**Robin Vale**
