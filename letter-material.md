# Letter material — claims, evidence and tried phrasings

Raw material for cover letters. This is **not** a master letter to cut down. A CV is a stock, where tailoring is selection — hence [the master CV](cv/master.md). A letter is an argument to one reader, and a superset to cut from gives exactly the template feeling that letters sharing paragraphs word for word have.

What is invariant from letter to letter is not the paragraphs. It is the claims. Each claim stands here with its evidence and the phrasing that has worked best so far.

**The phrasings are material, not text to paste.** Reuse the claim, rewrite the sentence for the reader.

## Sobriety is the service

**Claim:** The value lies as much in turning an AI project down as in building it, and an independent voice is the only one that can afford to say so.

**Evidence:** Advises boards and management without selling a platform. A published article series on reliability and operations rather than hype.

**Tried phrasing:** *"Sometimes the answer is AI, sometimes it is not, and much of the value lies in daring to say the latter."*

## Builds and advises — both, not one

**Claim:** The combination is rare. Most people who can build it cannot explain it to a board, and the other way round.

**Evidence:** Lead AI architect at a regional news publisher with a system in daily operation, alongside board advisory through an own company. RAG built into a leasing company's existing fleet management system.

**Tried phrasing:** *"At the same time, I build."* — short, and effective as the turn between the advisory and the building paragraph.

## Most of an AI system is not the model

**Claim:** The value sits in the deterministic frame around the model, which gives it direction and makes the result verifiable instead of hiding it in a black box.

**Evidence:** Thousands of lines of shell scripting as exactly that frame, readable by both the business and IT. Python for data processing.

**Tried phrasing:** *"…so the rules are readable and can be reviewed by both the business and IT, and so the model gets a direction and the results can be verified, instead of everything hiding in a black box."*

## Traceability is not a feature

**Claim:** If a claim cannot be traced back to its source, the output is not knowledge.

**Evidence:** Source grounding in the editorial system, internal RAG for semantic search, a monitoring system where every claim can be traced.

**Tried phrasing:** *"Without it, such a system is not intelligence but noise with confidence."* — the sharpest sentence in the collection. Swap the domain word; the form holds.

## Explaining is the trade, not a side skill

**Claim:** Two decades of getting into an unfamiliar field, weighing the sources and passing it on to people who have to act on it. The advisor who does not need translating.

**Evidence:** MSc in chemistry and trained science journalist. Editor, author, former board member of a national science writers' association. Has taught universities and hospitals to communicate their research. Course leader for staff, management and boards.

**Tried phrasing:** *"…without selling more technology than is needed."*

## Build on, do not tear down

**Claim:** Works by building on what already works and setting the direction together with the team.

**Evidence:** Integrations into existing platforms rather than greenfield: the leasing company's fleet system, a CMS through its JSON API, a social network's publishing API.

**Tried phrasing:** *"I prefer to build on what already works and set the direction with the team, not to tear it down."*

## Has taught business people, not only advised them

**Claim:** Has stood in front of a paying room and had to make AI useful in five hours, not only written about it.

**Evidence:** Course days run in partnership with a digital agency, at most five participants per instructor.

**Tried phrasing:** *"I have run course days for people who paid to be there — that is a different discipline from writing about the subject."*

### Numbers from a partnership — only with the partner beside you

Joint marketing material counted tasks and texts produced across both partners' clients. Most of them were the partner's. **They are not evidence in a letter written in your own name.** Without the partner beside them they are not a tight claim but a wrong one, and that is the kind that cannot be taken back if someone asks about it in an interview. They belong in shared sales material, where both names stand.

## The opening paragraph

The one paragraph never reused. Every letter so far opens with the recipient, not the applicant, and the best do it with something that could only be written about them: living near the museum and visiting often, or the posting's own best sentence quoted back. If the opening would work with another company's name, it is not finished.
